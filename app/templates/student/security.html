Biometric Privacy & Security Policy

Zero Biometric Storage

AttendX uses the W3C WebAuthn standard. Raw fingerprint images never leave your hardware. Only cryptographic public key signatures are validated.

5-Minute Window Binding

Pre-verifying 5 minutes prior to class proves your authentic identity ahead of time. Attendance is recorded only after the rotating QR is scanned.

Replay & Proxy Prevention

Challenge nonces and origin validation prevent replay attacks and proxy scanning between peers.

Accessible Fallback

Students using devices without biometric sensors can complete standard verification using institutional credentials.
